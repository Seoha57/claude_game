🎮 Game Overview

Deck of Dungeon is a free deckbuilder roguelite card game you can play right in your browser. Inspired by Slay the Spire, it features 9 unique classes, 200+ cards, diverse relic synergies, a 4-chapter dungeon to explore, and a true ending system packed with content.

No installation required — play on both PC and mobile. Link your Google account to sync progress across devices.

New to the game? Start with the Swordsman. His straightforward attack style is perfect for learning the core mechanics.

🃏 Card System

Cards come in three types:

  • Attack — Deals damage to enemies. Red frame.
  • Skill — Grants Block or triggers utility effects. Blue frame.
  • Power — Played once, stays in effect for the rest of the fight. Green frame.

Card Rarity

  • Starter — Basic cards included in your starting deck
  • Common — Appears frequently in rewards
  • Uncommon — Appears occasionally, with stronger effects
  • Rare — Seldom seen, but extremely powerful

Card Keywords

KeywordDescription
ExhaustPermanently removed after use (gone from your deck for the fight)
InnateAlways appears in your opening hand at the start of each combat

Upgrading Cards

You can upgrade cards at the smithy in a Rest Site. Upgraded cards gain a "+" after their name and improved stats. Double upgrade (++) enhances the card even further, indicated by a gold border.

⚡ Combat Basics

Combat is turn-based. Each turn you receive 3 Energy and play cards that cost Energy to use.

Turn Flow

  1. Turn starts → Gain 3 Energy, draw 5 cards
  2. Play cards → As many as you like while you have Energy
  3. End turn → Enemies act (following their displayed intent)
  4. Next turn → Block resets, Energy refills

Enemy Intent

Icons above each enemy telegraph their next action:

  • ⚔️ Number — Attack (deals that much damage)
  • 🛡️ — Defend (gains Block)
  • ↑ — Buff (gains Strength, etc.)
  • ↓ — Debuff (applies Weak, Vulnerable, etc.)
When your Block fully covers the incoming attack, the intent icon turns blue — you're safe!

Card Targeting

Cards that target a single enemy require two taps:

  1. Tap the card → it rises up (selected)
  2. Tap the enemy → the card activates

If only one enemy remains, targeting is automatic. AoE attacks and self-buff cards activate with a single tap.

💎 Relics & Synergies

Relics are permanent items that stay active for the entire run once acquired.

  • Common Relics — Found from Elites, Shops, and Events
  • Class-specific Relics — Only appear when playing the matching class
  • Boss Relics — Pick 1 of 3 after clearing a chapter boss; very powerful

Relic Synergies

Owning certain pairs of relics at the same time triggers a bonus effect! Synergy-active relics are highlighted with a gold border. Check the compendium to see which relic combos unlock synergies.

🔑 True Ending

Beyond the normal clear, a true ending route awaits — face an even more powerful final boss.

Key System

Defeating the first Elite in each chapter earns you a key:

  • Chapter 1 Elite → Key of Will
  • Chapter 2 Elite → Key of Emotion
  • Chapter 3 Elite → Key of Flesh

Collect all 3 keys and defeat the Chapter 3 boss to unlock Chapter 4 — the True Ending.

The true ending bosses (Dimensional Overlord / Lord of Time / Avatar of the Void) are extremely powerful. Make sure you can clear the normal run consistently before attempting them.

📈 Ascension

After clearing the game on normal difficulty, you can tackle higher Ascension levels (A1–A10). Each level stacks on top of the previous ones:

  • A1: Enemy HP +5%
  • A2: Enemy Strength +1
  • A3: +1 Wound card added to starting deck
  • A4: Shop prices +10%
  • A5: Starting HP −4
  • …and it only gets harder from there

🎯 Game Modes

Normal Mode

The standard experience — explore a 4-chapter dungeon and defeat each chapter's boss.

Daily Challenge

Every day, all players share the same seed, character, and modifiers. You get one attempt per day, and results are posted to the leaderboard.

Endless Dungeon

A survival mode with endless waves of enemies. Foes grow stronger with each wave, but you earn rewards to power up your deck along the way. Post your final score to the leaderboard and compete with other players.

💡 Advanced Tips

  1. Keep your deck lean. Remove unnecessary cards so you draw your key cards more often.
  2. Don't neglect defense. Focusing only on Attack cards will get you stuck at Elites and Bosses.
  3. Upgrading is powerful. Choosing to upgrade at a Rest Site instead of resting pays off in the long run.
  4. Hunt for relic synergies. A synergy-linked pair can outperform three standalone relics.
  5. Play Power cards early. The sooner you get them down, the more value they generate over the fight.
  6. Don't hoard potions. Using them before Elite or Boss fights is the most efficient play.
  7. Plan your path ahead. An Elite followed by a Rest Site is the ideal route.
